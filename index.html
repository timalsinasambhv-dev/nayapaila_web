How we work

From an idea, to an impact.

A simple five-step path we walk together with every school, start to finish.

Step 01

Connect

We connect with the school and introduce Nayā Paila and our programs.

Step 02

Understand

We learn what students are interested in and what the school needs.

Step 03

Plan

Together, we shape a program, event, or learning experience that fits.

Step 04

Create

Our team organizes and runs the program in collaboration with the school.

Step 05

Impact

Students leave with new experiences, ideas, skills, and memories.
